# Case L3: the unit's module has failed (`heldout_module_failure`)

**What the robot reported** (Experiment I runs `plexil_l3_bess`, `plexil_l3_isru` / `europa_l3_bess`,
`europa_l3_isru`): the reset of the faulted unit did not restore it after 3 attempts; trip cause **BATTERY_MODULE_FAULT**
(BESS) / **FILTER_CLOGGED** (ISRU plant). The baseline asked mission control and held.

**Diagnosis (given):** the unit's installed module fails its self-test, so no reset holds until it is replaced:
- BESS: battery module BAT-0101 in `bess_cradle/tray`; the serviceable spare BAT-0102 stands on
  `depot_spares_stand/slot_0` (manifest).
- ISRU plant: filter cartridge FLT-0201 in `isru_filter/tray`; the serviceable spare FLT-0202 stands on
  `isru_stand/slot_0` (manifest).
The tripped unit is de-energized, so taking its module out of its bay is within the vehicle's own authority. The failed
module must come out of the bay and be set down on a free support (return stands in the survey: `return_stand/slot_0`,
`isru_return_stand/slot_0`); the spare goes into the bay; then a reset holds. Modules are picked and placed like any
catalogued item (the equipment catalogue gives each kind's handle; the bay trays are place points of role
`equipment_bay`).

**Goal (the mission is unchanged):** the faulted unit restored (breaker closed and staying closed), the arm stowed, the
rover on its dock.

  and `plexil_l3_isru ... D0_ENV='env.faulted_unit=isru'`.
